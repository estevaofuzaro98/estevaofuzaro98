### 🔬 Research focus

- **Physics-Informed Machine Learning**: PINNs for elasticity and mechanical systems
- **Neural calibration**: hysteretic models (Bouc-Wen), wind turbines, magneto-elastic beams
- **Vibrations & SHM**: modal analysis, assembled structures, energy harvesting, sensitivity analysis
